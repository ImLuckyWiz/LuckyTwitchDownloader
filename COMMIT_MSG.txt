Download sections by whole HLS segments and use their real start for markers (v1.5.2)

ffmpeg's -ss on Twitch HLS VODs landed on a segment up to 10 s before the
requested time, but the app assumed the file started exactly at that time,
so chapters and Premiere markers were shifted.

- Read the VOD's media playlist (yt-dlp -g), pick the segments covering the
  part ourselves and give ffmpeg a local playlist with only those (no -ss)
- The real start/end (segment boundaries) now go to chapters and markers
- Exact mode: download aligned, then re-encode-cut from the real start
- Fall back to the old method (with a log warning) for encrypted, live,
  multi-URL or otherwise unsupported playlists; cancel is never swallowed
- Add tests/test_hls_sections.py
- Release notes for v1.5.2 (only this version)
